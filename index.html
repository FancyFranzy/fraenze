How we work

With the animal, never against it.

Relaxed animals give better takes. That is why their wellbeing comes first on every set.

  1. 01

    Trust, patience, reward

    Only positive, animal-friendly methods. We build every behaviour with reward, never with pressure.

  2. 02

    Natural behaviour

    We work with what the animal offers on its own and shape it into the shot. That is what makes it look real on camera.

  3. 03

    Calm and safe on set

    A stress-free, safe environment and breaks whenever the animal needs them. If something is too much, we say so.

Not on the list?
Brief us anyway.

Tell us the scene, not just the species. We source through keepers we know personally, assess every animal ourselves and prepare it for your shoot. If something isn’t safe or would be too much for the animal, we won’t do it. We tell you honestly what works, and propose what plays on camera and is right for the animal.

Fränze came to film in 2006, with her Weimaraner Mylo. Since that first job, animals have been her team on set, and they have always been her friends and family.

In twenty years she has built a wide network of keepers and trainers across Germany. Productions book her because she tells them honestly what is possible, and then delivers it.
